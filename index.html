Driven States Registry

A curated, openly graded record of material states that exist only while a drive is running — light-induced phases and Floquet-engineered bands. These are not eigenstates of the undriven Hamiltonian, which is why equilibrium materials databases cannot represent them. Every entry carries its drive parameters, lifetime and an explicit evidence level.

Provenance. Literature search and field extraction were performed by AI agents under human direction. Every numerical value and citation was checked against the cited work and every DOI verified — but no entry has been reviewed by a domain expert. peer_reviewed: false holds for all 82 states. Treat this as a structured, fully sourced starting point, not as a reviewed reference. Corrections are the intended path to improvement; each entry carries its sources so any claim can be checked at the source.
